## Duplication worth knowing about

- `isAuthenticated(req)` is copy-pasted identically into `app/api/finance/route.ts`, `app/api/finance/[id]/route.ts`, and `app/api/export/route.ts` rather than shared from `lib/auth.ts` - see `03-auth-and-middleware.mdx`.
- The `finance_entries` `CREATE TABLE` statement is duplicated between `lib/db.ts`'s `initDb()` and `scripts/setup-db.js` - see `04-finance-api.mdx`.
- `middleware.ts` re-implements its own `secret`/`jwtVerify` call rather than importing `verifyToken` from `lib/auth.ts`, even though both do the same thing with the same environment variable.
